Privacy for project inquiries

When you submit the form, Fernely Dev receives your name, email, service of interest and message. Phone is optional. If you visit from a campaign, we may also store its campaign name to understand where inquiries come from.

We use these details only to reply and discuss your project. They are stored on the private server space of the hosting account and only Fernely Dev can access them. Do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive information.

Inquiries are kept only as long as needed to reply and follow up. You can request access or deletion at any time by emailing fernelydev@gmail.com.

This form does not request card information or process payments.
